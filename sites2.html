Ancient Indian Heritage Sites

Taj Mahal

A white marble mausoleum built by Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal.

Hampi

Ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire, known for massive temples and stone chariots.

Ajanta Caves

Buddhist rock-cut caves famous for intricate paintings and sculptures.

Khajuraho Temples

Intricate carvings depicting spirituality, love, and war.

Fatehpur Sikri

A 16th-century Mughal city built by Akbar.

Mehrangarh Fort

One of the largest forts in India, built by Rao Jodha.

Nalanda University

One of the oldest universities in the world, dating back to the 5th century.

Golconda Fort

Known for its acoustic engineering and diamond trade.

Elephanta Caves

Rock-cut temples on Elephanta Island, dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Pattadakal

Chalukyan-era temples blending Dravidian and Nagara architecture.

Lothal

Indus Valley site with one of the world’s earliest dockyards.

Tawang Monastery

The largest Buddhist monastery in India, founded in the 17th century.
