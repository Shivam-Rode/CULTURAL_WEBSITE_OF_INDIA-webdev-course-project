Ancient Indian Engineering Marvels

Ellora Caves

Built using advanced rock-cutting technology, the Kailasa Temple was carved from a single rock using a top-down excavation method.

Konark Sun Temple

Astronomically aligned temple with giant sundials and interlocking architecture reinforced with iron clamps.

Brihadeeswarar Temple

Constructed using interlocking stone technology, the temple features a massive granite dome without any binding material.

Rani ki Vav

Stepwell designed with advanced water conservation techniques and intricate carvings showcasing ancient engineering brilliance.

Victoria Memorial

Built using a mix of Indo-Saracenic architecture, incorporating advanced dome engineering and marble construction.

Meenakshi Temple

Known for its towering gopurams, intricate sculptures, and earthquake-resistant foundation.

Sanchi Stupa

Hemispherical dome built using dry masonry construction techniques to ensure stability.

Mahabalipuram Temples

Rock-cut temples with water-resistant foundations and erosion-resistant stone architecture.

Iron Pillar of Delhi

Rust-proof iron metallurgy used to construct a 1,600-year-old iron pillar that has withstood time.
