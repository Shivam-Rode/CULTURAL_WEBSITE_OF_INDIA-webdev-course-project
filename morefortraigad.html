Throne

The golden throne (Raj Sinhasan) of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was a symbol of his sovereignty. It was ornately designed and placed in the Raigad Fort’s royal court (Durbar), where his coronation took place in 1674.

Nagar Khana Darwaja

This is the main entrance to the royal court at Raigad Fort. "Nagarkhana" refers to a space where drums and musical instruments were played to announce royal events and the arrival of dignitaries.

Samadi

Located at Raigad Fort, the samadhi (memorial) marks the final resting place of Shivaji Maharaj. It is built near the Jagadishwar Temple, honoring his legacy and contributions to the Maratha Empire

jagadiswar Temple

A temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, situated near Shivaji Maharaj’s samadhi at Raigad Fort. The temple reflects his deep faith and patronage of Hindu traditions.

Maha Darwaja

The grand entrance of Raigad Fort, built with massive stone blocks. It served as a secure gateway, designed to be impenetrable against enemy attacks.
